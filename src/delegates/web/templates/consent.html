Before you start

This study looks at how people argue about contested policy questions, and at how well an AI system can represent a person's views.

What happens

  • You answer questions about yourself and your general outlook (~15 min).
  • You have a conversation about one policy question with an AI that argues the other side, marking where you stand after each exchange (~30 min).
  • You are invited back twice over the following weeks for two shorter sessions, each paid separately.

Your data

  • Your answers and the conversation are recorded and stored under your participant ID, not your name.
  • You can stop at any point and still be paid for the time you gave.
